Hello! I am an Assistant Professor in the Biostatistics Program, Public Health Sciences Division at Fred Hutchinson Cancer Center. The overarching goal of my research is to design clinical trials that are flexible, adaptable, and efficient, while maintaining a focus on statistical rigor, clinical relevance, and patient safety. I am especially motivated by settings in which trials face practical (e.g., small populations) or scientific complexity (e.g., novel immunotherapies).

I am particularly interested in oncology clinical trials. At Fred Hutch, I am involved with the SWOG Cancer Research Network. I also collaborate with Seattle Children's on clinical trials in pediatric cell and gene therapies. I completed the [FDA-OCE-ASA Educational Fellowship](https://community.amstat.org/fdaoceasaoncologyeducationalfellowship/home) in oncology drug development and regulatory policy.

I received my PhD from the [Department of Biostatistics](https://www.biostat.washington.edu) at the University of Washington, advised by [Dr. Ting Ye](https://sites.google.com/view/tingye). A core theme of my methodological work has been covariate adjustment to increase efficiency in randomized trials.
*Our package [RobinCar](https://cran.r-project.org/web/packages/RobinCar/index.html) for covariate adjustment in randomized clinical trials &ndash; and its sister package [RobinCar2](https://cran.r-project.org/web/packages/RobinCar2/index.html) &ndash; are now available on CRAN!*
